Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

The short version

FailMore doesn’t collect, store or share any of your data, everything you log stays on your phone

What FailMore stores

Your workouts, sets, goals, plans, equipment and settings are saved on your device so the app works, none of it is sent to us or anyone else

Accounts, ads and tracking

There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third party tracking tools in FailMore

Backups

If you export a backup, the file is saved where you choose, FailMore never uploads it

Purchases

If you buy FailMore on the App Store, Apple handles the payment, FailMore never sees your payment details

Questions

Reach out through the FailMore GitHub page and you’ll get an answer from the person who made it
