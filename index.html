How FailMore works
  1. Step 1

    Find a weight where you fail on the last few reps of your last set

  2. Step 2

    Set a goal to do all of those reps without failing

  3. Step 3

    Reach that goal, increase the weight, and fail some more

That’s failing upward

It’s also called progressive overload
